Formula
P = W / t
W = mgh
Power measures the rate of doing work. For the same work, less time means greater power.

Compare Two Motors

Both motors lift the same load to the same height. Their power differs because of the time taken.

Press “Compare two motors” to see a numerical comparison.

Observation & Learning

Work: force × displacement in the force direction.

Power: work done per unit time.

SI unit: watt (W); 1 W = 1 J/s.

Key concept: Increasing the load or lifting height increases work and power when time is fixed.
