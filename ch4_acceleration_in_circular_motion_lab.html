Centripetal Acceleration
Even when speed is constant, velocity changes because direction changes. Therefore the object has acceleration directed toward the centre.
ac = v² / r
Increase speed and acceleration rises rapidly because speed is squared. Increase radius and acceleration decreases.
Key ideas:
1. Constant Speed

The speed may remain unchanged during uniform circular motion.
2. Changing Direction

Velocity changes continuously because its direction changes.
3. Inward Acceleration

Centripetal acceleration always points toward the centre.
4. Formula Effect

a₍c₎ ∝ v² and a₍c₎ ∝ 1/r.
