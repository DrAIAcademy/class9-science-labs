Second Equation of Motion
s = ut + ½at²

This equation gives the displacement of an object moving with constant acceleration.

At t = 0 s: s = 0
Understand the equation step by step:
1. Initial Velocity

u contributes the displacement ut.
2. Acceleration

a adds an extra displacement due to changing velocity.
3. Time Squared

The acceleration term depends on t², so its effect grows rapidly with time.
4. Total Displacement

s = ut + ½at² combines both contributions.
