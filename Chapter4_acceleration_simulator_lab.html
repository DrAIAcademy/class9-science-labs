Acceleration tells us how quickly velocity changes with time.
a = (vf − vi) ÷ t
For constant acceleration:
vf = vi + at
Δx = vit + ½at²
Positive acceleration → velocity increases in the positive direction.
Try these ready experiments:
1. Speeding Up
Start from rest and accelerate forward.
2. Slowing Down
Positive velocity with negative acceleration.
3. Constant Velocity
No acceleration, so velocity stays constant.
Observe the arrows: the cyan arrow shows velocity and the green arrow shows acceleration direction. If acceleration is opposite to velocity, the object slows down first.
