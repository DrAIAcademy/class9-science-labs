Observation & Learning

Constant force: Work = area of rectangle = F × s.

Increasing force: Work = area of triangle = ½ × Fmax × s.

Decreasing force: Work = area of triangle = ½ × Fmax × s.

Opposing force: Area below the axis gives negative work.

SI unit: joule (J) = newton metre (N·m).
