Third Equation of Motion

Concept breakdown:
Initial velocity — u

The velocity of the object at the beginning.
Acceleration — a

The constant rate at which velocity changes.
Displacement — s

The change in position over which acceleration acts.
Final velocity — v

Calculated from the square root of u² + 2as.
