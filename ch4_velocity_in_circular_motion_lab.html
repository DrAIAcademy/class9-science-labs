Velocity in Circular Motion
Velocity is always tangent to the circular path. Its magnitude may remain constant, but its direction changes continuously.
v = ωr
ω = v / r
The velocity vector is perpendicular to the radius at every point on the circle.
Key ideas:
1. Speed

Speed is the magnitude of velocity and can remain constant.
2. Direction

The direction of velocity changes at every point on the circle.
3. Tangential Velocity

The velocity vector is always tangent to the circular path.
4. Angular Speed

Angular speed tells how quickly the object sweeps through angle.
